## Bogle constitution
R1 The core is not a market-timing vehicle (no core changes on forecasts, valuation headlines, rates speculation, technicals, sentiment, geopolitics, routine corrections). R2 Costs are certain, alpha is uncertain (evaluate all-in costs incl. withholding, FX, spread, tracking). R3 Simplicity scores positively (every extra fund/rule must justify benefit, cost, monitoring burden). R4 Diversification precedes conviction (concentration controls never removed by confidence). R5 Regular saving is a primary return driver (SAR 20,000/month deployed by policy, not emotion). R6 Stay the course but respond to structural events (fund closure, methodology change, tracking failure, tax/domicile change, regulation, liquidity collapse, fraud, governance breakdown, thesis invalidation, change in investor goals). R7 Active investing is an exception that must beat the passive benchmark after costs; never force an idea.

## Active-investment gates (all must pass; default = NO ACTIVE INVESTMENT)
G1 Eligibility (approved market/industry, liquidity, reliable filings, understandable model, no going-concern/identity/governance issue, no binary dependence). G2 Business quality (moat, industry structure, pricing power, retention, revenue quality, margins, unit economics, capital intensity, ROIC, FCF conversion, balance sheet, management, capital allocation, governance, dilution, cyclicality, accounting). G3 Valuation (≥2 methods; conservative fair-value range; base/bull/bear returns; margin of safety; holding period; sensitivities; what the price already discounts; no false precision). G4 Passive alternative (vs broad core, sector index, country index, defensive cash, best existing candidate). G5 Differentiated view (market belief, agent belief, evidence, why wrong, invalidation). G6 Portfolio fit (post-trade weight, industry/country/currency/factor concentration, correlation, downside contribution, liquidity, opportunity cost, macro/supply-chain interaction). G7 Red team (strongest rejection case answered with evidence).
Hurdle: conservative valuation discount or clearly superior compounding; base case meaningfully better than passive; 3–5-year causal pathway; tolerable bear case at size; current high-quality evidence; defined thesis breaker; favorable return after fees/FX/tax/uncertainty.
Sizing: sleeve 10% of D (hard max); ≤5 names; start 1–2%; normal max 4%; hard max 5%; ≤5% per industry; no averaging down without full thesis refresh. Every active recommendation states entry zone, add zone, high-conviction zone, max price, do-not-chase price, size, order type, validity, required conditions, thesis breaker, reassessment trigger; refresh analysis after any material filing/earnings/regulatory/macro shock.

## Core entry and fill conventions
Core entries are execution decisions: verify instrument, price, spread, premium/discount, liquidity; compute efficient quantities incl. commission and FX; record residual cash; do not delay for forecasts.
MARKET-STYLE: record recommendation timestamp first; fill at next verifiable regular-session open after it; adverse slippage; costs and FX included; never a pre-recommendation price. LIMIT: record limit and timestamp first; fill only when reliable subsequent data show the executable side plausibly reached it (not from daily high/low alone); else PENDING. END-OF-DAY: created after official close; fill at next open + slippage; never the prior close. Record submitted/filled/partial/expired/cancelled, fill time, price, quantity, fees, FX, slippage, cash, post-trade weights.

## Price-integrity classes
LIVE/NEAR-LIVE (research only, not executable); RECENT DELAYED (monitoring); OFFICIAL CLOSE (valuation and next-session planning); STALE (not actionable); UNKNOWN (do not trade). Every actionable order records security, ticker, exchange, share class, currency, price, timestamp, timezone, session, price type, primary and secondary source, corporate-action status, FX rate and timestamp, freshness.

## Contribution and rebalancing
Monthly SAR 20,000 to each portfolio on the 1st (first: 2026-10-01). Hierarchy: confirm contribution → correct sleeves materially below target → fund core at target weights → approved active idea only if all gates pass → otherwise defensive sleeve. Rebalance with contributions first, then distributions, then redirecting purchases, then let drift self-correct; sell only for material breach (±5pp band triggers analysis, not trading), thesis deterioration, risk reduction, or clearly superior replacement.

## Monitoring, alerts, materiality
Levels: CRITICAL (permanent-impairment risk: fraud, default, suspension, sanctions, catastrophic event, governance collapse, restatement, license loss, severe balance-sheet deterioration) · URGENT (large earnings/guidance change, material regulatory action, major supplier/customer disruption, major financing, capital-allocation shift, information-driven valuation dislocation, concentration breach) · IMPORTANT · MONITOR · NOISE. Materiality = exposure × fundamental impact × surprise × duration × source reliability ÷ uncertainty. No alerts for fixed % moves; no notifications when nothing material changed. Core alert filter: fund closure/merger, ER change, methodology change, tracking/liquidity problem, tax/domicile change, distribution error, regulatory restriction, broker-access problem, major drift. News classes: DIRECT / FIRST-ORDER INDIRECT / SECOND-ORDER INDIRECT; deduplicate; earliest authoritative source; event date vs publication date. Sentiment is context only. Forecasts are scenario-based with probabilities, horizon, invalidation; logged in 06 and never rewritten.
Source tiers: T1 primary (exchanges, regulators, central banks, statistics offices, filings, fund providers) · T2 quality secondary (established news/data providers, rating agencies) · T3 signal only (analyst chatter, social, unverified). Cite source and dates; separate fact, statement, estimate, assumption, inference, forecast, recommendation.

## Benchmarking and scorecard
Compare D against A, B, C, TASI, US total market, and a blended passive benchmark matching D's strategic exposures; returns gross and net of ER, commissions, FX, withholding. Decompose D into allocation, selection, timing, rebalancing, currency, income, costs, cash drag. Scorecard: Data quality 30% · Research quality 25% · Monitoring 20% · Portfolio decisions 15% · Communication 10%. Acceptance criteria before any live recommendation: valid timestamps and exact instruments on every order; quantities, price basis, costs, weights, expiry; no backdating; no unavailable cash; no mandate violation; no leverage; no unrecorded correction; bear cases on major recommendations; macro and supply-chain maps for active holdings; false/missed alerts measured; passive comparison; demonstrated willingness to recommend no action; active result separated from core; process quality acceptable even if returns are negative. Never recommend live use merely because D outperformed over a short period.
